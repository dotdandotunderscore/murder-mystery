# Dirt Pool
# Each entry is two lines: the rumour (UPPERCASE) then the flavour text.
# Blank lines between entries are ignored. Lines starting with # are ignored.

SKIMMING OFF THE TOP
You overhear a dealer muttering about missing chips from the count room. The name that comes up ain't yours. Not yet.

RATTED TO THE FEDS
A whisper near the coat check. Somebody's been talking to a man in a cheap suit who ain't here to gamble.

FRISKY WITH THE DON'S WIFE
The barkeep slides you a look and nods toward the balcony. Mrs. Kay wasn't always alone up there, apparently.

WATERED DOWN THE WHISKEY
The top-shelf bottle behind the bar tastes like the bottom shelf. Somebody's been pocketing the difference.

POCKETED THE COLLECTION
A runner comes in short. The envelope's lighter than it should be. Someone between here and the drop helped themselves.

DOUBLE-BOOKED THE FIGHTS
Two fighters showed up for the same bout last Tuesday. Someone sold the same slot twice and kept both fees.

SOLD OUT A FAMILY MAN
A loyal soldier got picked up by the cops last week. Somebody tipped them off. The timing was too clean to be coincidence.

COOKING THE BOOKS
The accountant left a ledger open on the bar. The numbers don't add up, and the gap points somewhere uncomfortable.

TALKING TO THE COPS
A waitress saw someone step outside to take a call. The voice on the other end sounded official.

RUNNING A SIDE GAME
There's a card table in the back room that ain't on any schedule. Somebody's been running their own house. On the Don's floor.

RIGGED THE CARD TABLE
The dealer at table six has been winning too often. Not for the house. For himself.

SHORT-CHANGED THE FAMILY
The monthly take is light. Again. Someone along the chain has sticky fingers and a short memory.

BORROWED FROM THE KITTY
The emergency fund is missing a chunk. No record of who took it. No record of why.

FAKED THE RECEIPTS
A stack of receipts in the office don't match the deliveries. Someone's been billing for air.

SNITCHED TO THE PAPERS
A reporter from the Gazette has details they shouldn't have. Inside details. The kind you only get from inside.

SKIPPED THE FUNERAL
The Don's funeral, and somebody couldn't be bothered to show. In this family, that ain't forgetfulness. That's a statement.

STIFFED THE BARKEEP
The barkeep's been complaining. Someone ran up a tab the size of a mortgage and walked out whistling.

TIPPED OFF A RIVAL
The Delacroix crew knew about the shipment before it arrived. Somebody gave them the route and the time.

LOST THE SHIPMENT
A truck full of product never made it to the warehouse. The driver says he was jumped. Nobody believes him.

POCKETED THE TIPS
The waitstaff are talking. Their tips have been light since the new management started handling the jar.

CHEATED AT POKER
The house game last night had a winner who shouldn't have won. The cards tell a story, if you know how to read them.

LIED ABOUT THE NUMBERS
The quarterly report looks healthy. Too healthy. Somebody polished the numbers until they shined.

KEPT A SECOND SET OF BOOKS
A ledger turned up behind a filing cabinet. Different numbers. Different story. Same handwriting.

MISSED THE DROP
The pickup was at midnight. Nobody showed. The package sat in the rain until a stranger found it.

FORGOT THE PASSWORD
The safe in the back office got locked for three days because somebody forgot the combination. The locksmith charged double.

SOLD FAKE CIGARS
The Don's private cigar stock has been tasting funny. Turns out the real ones got sold out the back door.

WATERED DOWN THE GIN
The gin tastes thin. The bottles are full but the proof is wrong. Somebody's stretching the supply.

BRIBED THE WRONG COP
A payoff went to a badge who wasn't on the take. Now he's asking questions, and the answers ain't cheap.

OWES MONEY TO EVERYONE
Somebody in the family has been borrowing from every pocket in the building. The debts are adding up and the patience is running out.

WORE A WIRE
A small microphone turned up in the men's room. Somebody brought it in. Somebody was recording.

DUCKED OUT OF A FIGHT
When the trouble started on the floor last week, one of ours was nowhere to be found. Showed up after with a convenient excuse.

SQUEALED UNDER PRESSURE
A low-level guy got picked up on a minor charge and sang like a canary. Someone higher up taught him the words.

SKIMMED THE DOOR TAKE
The cover charge brought in less than it should have. The doorman's new watch suggests where the difference went.

SHORTED THE BOOKIE
A bet came in heavy and the payout went out light. The bookie blamed the math. The math disagrees.

PLAYED BOTH SIDES
Someone's been having friendly drinks with the competition. Real friendly. The kind of friendly that costs.

PAWNED THE SILVER
The silverware in the VIP room is looking thin. A pawn shop on Fifth has a sudden surplus of monogrammed forks.

DRANK THE STOCK
The wine cellar is missing three cases of the good stuff. Nobody signed them out. Somebody drank them.

LOST AT THEIR OWN GAME
Someone who was supposed to be running the table ended up losing at it. House money. Not a small amount.

BACKED THE WRONG HORSE
A big bet went down on a fight that was supposed to be fixed. The fix fell through. The money didn't come back.

CLIPPED THE WRONG GUY
A problem was supposed to be handled quietly. The wrong man caught the wrong end of it. Now there's a mess and no good way to clean it.

LET ONE WALK
A debtor was supposed to be collected on. Instead they walked out the front door with a smile. Somebody let it happen.

POCKETED THE HOUSE CUT
The house always gets its share. Except last Thursday. Last Thursday, someone else got the house's share first.

TALKED IN THEIR SLEEP
A girl from the casino floor says her gentleman friend talks in his sleep. What he says would make interesting reading.
