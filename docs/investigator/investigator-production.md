## Ghost-Host Actor Briefing

### Who They Are

A staff member playing the host of Mr. Sage's ghost. Identified by investigators via the Pale Flame at end of Part 2.

### Initial Scene

When an investigator says "Sage" (or similar):

1. Go glassy-eyed, change voice — deeper, older, afraid. Speak in fragments:
   - *"The cards... the cards were wrong..."*
   - *"A man with cold eyes... they called him... the Viper..."*
   - *"Numbers. I found the numbers. In the book. I shouldn't have taken it..."*
   - *"Green walls. Green room. He smiled at me. Then..."*
   - Trail off. Blink. Come back to yourself. Look confused.
2. Tell the player to type `the hunt begins` into the app.

### Ongoing Hints

Players can return anytime. Check their progress and nudge accordingly:

| Player seems... | Say something like... |
|---|---|
| Lost, no UV clues found | *"The cards... start at the tables... the light will show you..."* |
| Found the Viper, not the barkeep | *"Someone saw... at the bar... they watched him leave and me stay..."* |
| Found a few clues, missing the mirror | *"The dead remember in reflections... find a mirror..."* |
| Found most clues, missing the nest | *"His den... he keeps trophies of what he's done... look where you wouldn't think to..."* |
| Has all evidence, needs to trade | *"You know what happened. But knowing isn't enough. You need proof. The weapon. The ledger. His name. Others here have what you need..."* |
| Stuck on a red herring | *"That's not the way... you're chasing shadows... go back to what you know..."* |
| Has everything, ready for accusation | Give code phrase: `accusation` |

### General Demeanour

Fragmented, pained, fading in and out. Short bursts. Not fully aware of being dead. When not performing, just be normal staff — the ghost only surfaces when investigators approach.

## Cross-Faction Dependencies

### Clues the Investigator Needs (for accusation prompt)

| Clue | From Faction | How They Encounter It |
|---|---|---|
| **THE VIPER** | Mobsters | Vincent Vane is a figure in their storyline — handler, rival, or enforcer they work with |
| **JADE DAGGER** | Thieves | Found during their heist — a distinctive weapon, just loot to them |
| **BLACK LEDGER** | Occultists | Discovered through supernatural investigations — the ledger has occult significance beyond finances |

### Clues the Investigator Can Trade Away

| Clue | Suggested Recipient | Why They'd Want It |
|---|---|---|
| **BLOOD MONEY** | Mobsters | Financial evidence they want suppressed |
| **LIGHTER** or **NOTEBOOK** | Thieves | Practical tools |
| **TORN PAGE** | Occultists | Fragment of the ledger they want to complete |
| **MARKED CARDS** | Any | Evidence of rigged games |
