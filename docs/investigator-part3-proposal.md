# Investigator Part 3 — "Who Killed Mr. Sage?"

## Design Philosophy

Part 3 is structurally different from the thief's QR scavenger hunt. The thief *collects* — the investigator *deduces*. The UV trail builds narrative understanding (who, how, why), but the **proof** lives in other factions' inventories. The detective must figure out what evidence they need from context, then trade for it. The payoff is a Cluedo-style accusation prompt where the player clicks the right clues into the right blanks.

Three phases:
1. **Investigation** — UV clues form a loose web around the room. Some are code phrases that unlock pages with clues and narrative. Some are red herrings. Some are just atmosphere. Each real page hints where to look next, but players can find them in any order. The player follows leads, hits dead ends, builds a picture.
2. **Evidence gathering** — The detective now knows what happened but can't prove it. The key evidence is held by other factions. Time to trade.
3. **Accusation** — One prompt. Seven blanks. Click the right clues. Solve the murder.

**Mr. Sage's ghost** is a real person in the room (staff/actor playing the host). All ghost interactions are live — the player says "Sage" to the actor, the actor performs the fragmented testimony scene in character, then directs them to a code phrase that kicks off Part 3. After that, players can return to the ghost-host at any point for hints (see Ghost Briefing section below).

---

## The Murder (What Actually Happened)

Mr. Sage was an accountant. He came to the Broken Drum as a guest and quickly noticed the private card games were rigged — the house was cheating high-rollers and washing the profits through phantom winners. He took a page from the casino's shadow ledger as proof, then made the fatal mistake of trying to use it as leverage.

The casino's fixer — a man called **the Viper**, real name **Vincent Vane** — dealt with the problem. He lured Sage to the Green Room, killed him with a jade-handled dagger, and had the room cleaned. The staff were told to forget. The ledger page vanished. Business as usual.

## The Accusation (What the Detective Must Prove)

**Final prompt on the accusation page:**

> `_____ killed Mr. Sage with the _____ in the _____ because he found the _____. The _____ proves this wasn't his first victim. The _____ places him at the scene. And the _____ shows how they made it all disappear.`

**Answer:** THE VIPER, JADE DAGGER, GREEN ROOM, BLACK LEDGER, HIT LIST, BARKEEP'S NOTE, CLEANING ROTA

Seven blanks. A mix of clues the detective earns from UV investigation and clues they must trade for from other factions.

| Clue | Source | How obtained |
|---|---|---|
| THE VIPER | **Trade** (from Mobsters) | The killer's codename — the mob know their own people |
| JADE DAGGER | **Trade** (from Thieves) | The murder weapon — found during their heist, they don't know its significance |
| GREEN ROOM | **UV page** (`dead mans hand`) | The crime scene — detective already found the body here, UV page confirms it as the kill site |
| BLACK LEDGER | **Trade** (from Occultists) | What Sage found — the ledger has occult significance beyond just finances |
| HIT LIST | **UV page** (`the vipers nest`) | Vane's list of victims — Sage wasn't the first |
| BARKEEP'S NOTE | **UV page** (`last orders`) | Eyewitness testimony — the barkeep saw Vane enter and leave alone |
| CLEANING ROTA | **UV page** (`no witnesses`) | The cover-up schedule — proves the Green Room was scrubbed that night |

**3 clues from trading, 4 clues from UV investigation.** The detective earns the bulk of their case themselves but cannot close it without help.

## Page-by-Page Flow

### The Ghost (Real Person — Not Pagified)

**Mr. Sage's ghost** is a staff member / actor in the room. The host that the detective identified via the Pale Flame in Part 2. Players physically approach this person and speak to them.

**Page 42 — "The Hunt Begins"** (code: `the hunt begins`)

A short placeholder page. The ghost-host actor performs the testimony scene live (fragmented memories — the Viper, the rigged games, the ledger, the Green Room), then tells the player to type this code phrase. The page itself is a brief transition into the investigation:

*The dead have spoken. Now it's your turn. The Pale Flame shows what the living tried to hide. Use it. Search this place. The walls remember what the guilty want you to forget.*

- Requires flags: `found the shell of mr. sage`, `the pale flame`
- Grants flag: `sage spoke`
- Grants clue: TORN PAGE
- No text links (player must search with UV light)

**The code phrase comes from the actor, not from a previous page.** The ghost-host tells the player what to type after performing the scene.

### Investigation Pages (UV Trail)

---

**Page 43 — "The Viper's Table"** (code: `snake eyes`)

The Pale Flame reveals marks on the card table: tally marks, initials, a system. The house was cheating. Systematically, ruthlessly. High-rollers lured to private tables, fleeced clean, sent home too ashamed to complain.

Sage wasn't a gambler — he was an accountant. He saw the numbers for what they were the moment he sat down. The man who ran these tables: they called him the Viper. Slick, patient, cold-blooded. Ran the private games like a surgeon runs an operating theatre.

*Wherever there are crooked cards, there are crooked drinks to go with them. Maybe the bar knows something.*

- Requires flag: `the pale flame`
- Grants flag: `found the viper`
- Grants clue: MARKED CARDS
- Hints toward: bar area → `last orders`

---

**Page 44 — "The Barkeep's Burden"** (code: `last orders`)

Hidden near the bar. This wasn't written by the mob — this was left by someone who saw too much and couldn't live with it.

A barkeep. Working the private rooms that night. They saw the Viper go into the Green Room with Sage. Heard nothing — soundproofed walls. But saw Vane come out alone, and the cleaning crew go in after.

They wrote this in invisible ink because they couldn't say it out loud but they couldn't keep it inside either. A confession to an empty room.

*The barkeep saw him go in. Sage never came out. But where did they take what was left? There's always a door nobody uses.*

- Requires flag: `the pale flame`
- Grants flag: `heard the barkeep`
- Grants clue: BARKEEP'S NOTE
- Hints toward: a doorway / exit → `no witnesses`

---

**Page 45 — "Clean Up"** (code: `no witnesses`)

Near an exit. The words glow under the Pale Flame like a warning.

The Green Room was cleaned within the hour. The weapon — something sharp, something quiet — was disposed of. Staff were gathered and told, politely, that Mr. Sage had left in good health and good spirits. Anyone who remembered differently would be helped to forget. There's a schedule here — dates, rooms, names. A cleaning rota for crimes that never happened.

*Whoever cleaned that room missed the rug. They always miss something. And somewhere in this place, there's a dead man who remembers his own face. Find a mirror.*

- Requires flag: `the pale flame`
- Grants flag: `saw the cleanup`
- Grants clue: CLEANING ROTA
- Hints toward: a mirror / reflective surface → `dead mans hand`

---

**Page 46 — "The Last Hand"** (code: `dead mans hand`)

A mirror. The Pale Flame shows words on the glass — or behind it. A dead man's message to anyone with the eyes to see.

Sage's final night: he came back to the casino with the torn page from the ledger. Thought he had leverage — show the authorities, or the casino pays for silence. Classic mistake. You don't blackmail people who solve problems by making people disappear.

He sat across from the Viper in the Green Room. Showed him the evidence. The Viper smiled. Sage didn't leave that room alive.

The detective stares at the mirror. Their own reflection stares back.

*Something in this place has his secrets. Hidden where only a snake would go.*

- Requires flag: `the pale flame`
- Grants flag: `saw the last hand`
- Grants clue: GREEN ROOM
- Hints toward: somewhere concealed → `the vipers nest`

---

**Page 47 — "The Viper's Nest"** (code: `the vipers nest`)

The hardest to find. Hidden where you'd have to really look.

Initials: V.V. Vincent Vane. A list of names with lines through them — Sage isn't the first, and judging by the empty space at the bottom, he won't be the last.

Vane doesn't just run rigged card games. He's the casino's fixer. The mob points, and the Viper bites. Every problem that walked through those doors and didn't walk out again — Vane handled it.

The detective has the full picture: the motive (Sage found the ledger), the killer (Vincent Vane, the Viper), the method (the Green Room, a meeting that was never meant to end well). But knowing isn't proving.

*You need evidence. Real evidence. A name. A weapon. A book of sins. You don't have any of them — but someone in this casino does. Time to start trading favours.*

- Requires flags: `the pale flame`, `found the viper`
- Grants flag: `knows the truth`
- Grants clue: HIT LIST

---

**Page 48 — "Follow the Money"** (code: `blood money`)

Hidden figures on the wall. The Pale Flame reveals columns of numbers — dates, amounts, names. A shadow ledger, written where no audit would ever look. The casino was washing dirty money through the private games. Winners who never existed. Losses routed to accounts offshore.

Sage saw it. Accountant's eyes. He couldn't help himself — it's like asking a musician not to hear a wrong note. He took a page. One page. That was enough to get him killed.

*The money flows through this place like blood through a body. Dirty in, clean out. And anyone who notices the stain ends up like Sage.*

- Requires flag: `the pale flame`
- Grants flag: `followed the money`
- Grants clue: BLOOD MONEY

### Red Herring Pages

---

**Page 50 — "The Winning Streak"** (code: `lucky streak`)

A high-roller named Delacroix on an impossible winning streak. Suspicious? Absolutely. Connected to the murder? No.

Old marks near the roulette wheel — someone was tracking Delacroix's wins. The detective's instincts fire: *nobody's that lucky. Unless they're cheating. Unless they're in on it.*

But the trail goes cold. Delacroix is just a gambler who got lucky. Nothing more.

*Not every thread leads somewhere. Some just fray. Back to work.*

- Requires flag: `the pale flame`
- No useful flags or clues granted

---

**Page 51 — "House Rules"** (code: `house rules`)

Near a staff area. Internal protocols — shift changes, room access logs, cleaning schedules. Mundane. Bureaucratic. The detective skims it looking for something incriminating.

The cleaning schedule confirms the Green Room was "deep cleaned" that night — but the detective already knew that.

*Paperwork. The detective's natural enemy. This tells you nothing you didn't already know. Move on.*

- Requires flag: `the pale flame`
- No useful flags or clues granted

---

**Page 53 — "A Cold Trail"** (code: `cold deck`)

Under a chair. The Pale Flame reveals a name: **Marco Peretti**. Sounds important. Sounds connected.

The detective digs through what they know. Peretti... nothing. No link to Sage, no link to the Viper. Just a name on the underside of a chair in a casino full of secrets.

Maybe it meant something once. Maybe it means something to someone else. But it doesn't mean anything to you. Not tonight.

*Dead end. Literally. Some names are just names.*

- Requires flag: `the pale flame`
- No useful flags or clues granted
- (But MARCO PERETTI could be a hook for another faction's storyline, or pure noise)

The endgame. The detective lays out the case: every thread, every lie, every hidden mark on the walls of this casino. Three days ago they took a job finding a missing husband. Now they're standing in a den of thieves and murderers with a ghost's testimony and a magic torch, about to name a killer.

## Cross-Faction Trading

### What the detective needs and from whom

**THE VIPER** (from Mobsters)

*How mobsters encounter it:* The Viper / Vincent Vane is a figure in the mobster storyline — their handler, their rival, or someone they're ordered to work with. The clue THE VIPER surfaces naturally in their progression.

*What the detective offers:* BARKEEP'S NOTE — the mobsters want to know who's been talking. A witness is a loose end. (This creates moral tension: the detective trades a witness's safety for evidence.)

---

**JADE DAGGER** (from Thieves)

*How thieves encounter it:* During their heist, the thieves find a distinctive jade-handled dagger — in a display case, a locked room, wherever. It's loot to them. They don't know it's a murder weapon.

*What the detective offers:* LIGHTER, NOTEBOOK, or BLOOD MONEY — practical items or fenceable intel.

---

**BLACK LEDGER** (from Occultists)

*How occultists encounter it:* The ledger has occult significance — it records debts that go beyond money. Souls, favours, bindings. The occultist discovers it through their supernatural investigations, perhaps connected to why Sage's ghost is tethered.

*What the detective offers:* TORN PAGE — a fragment from the ledger the occultist wants to complete. Or MARKED CARDS — evidence of the games that generated the debts recorded in the ledger.

---

### Clues the detective doesn't need for the accusation (trade fodder)

These clues come from UV pages but aren't required for the final prompt. They're valuable as trade currency:

| Clue | From UV Page | Trade Value |
|---|---|---|
| TORN PAGE | Ghost (page 42) | Fragment of the ledger — occultists want it |
| MARKED CARDS | `snake eyes` (page 43) | Evidence of rigged games — could interest any faction |
| BLOOD MONEY | `blood money` (page 48) | Financial evidence — mobsters might want to suppress it, thieves might want to exploit it |

## Ghost Briefing (For the Actor)

The ghost-host is a real person. After the initial pagified contact (page 42), all further interactions are live. The actor should be briefed to give hints based on what the player has found. They can check the player's progress in the app or be briefed by a game runner.

**General ghost demeanour:** Fragmented, pained, fading in and out. Speaks in short bursts. Knows more than they can articulate. Not fully aware they're dead — they slip between memories of the night they died and awareness of the present.

**Hint tiers:**

| Player progress | Ghost nudges toward |
|---|---|
| Just started, no UV clues found | *"The cards... the cards were wrong... start at the tables..."* → `snake eyes` |
| Has found 1-2 UV clues | Nudge toward unfound clues based on the suggested chain. E.g. if they have `found the viper` but not `heard the barkeep`: *"Someone saw... at the bar... they watched him leave and me stay..."* |
| Has found most UV clues but not `the vipers nest` | *"His den... he keeps trophies... look where you wouldn't think to look..."* |
| Has `knows the truth` but missing trade clues | *"You know the story. But you need proof. The weapon. The ledger. His name. Others in this place have what you need."* |
| Has some trade clues but not all | Name what's missing: *"You still need the [weapon/ledger/name]... someone here has it..."* |
| Player is stuck on a red herring | *"That's not the way... you're chasing shadows... go back to what you know..."* |

The ghost can also respond to direct questions. If a player asks "who killed you?" the ghost can give fragmentary answers: *"The Viper... cold eyes... he smiled when he..."* — enough to confirm they're on the right track without giving away the full answer.

## Clue Inventory Progression

| Point | Clues Gained | Clues Lost (via trade) |
|---|---|---|
| Start of Part 3 | NOTEBOOK, PENCIL, LIGHTER | |
| Ghost scene → `the hunt begins` | + TORN PAGE | |
| UV: snake eyes | + MARKED CARDS | |
| UV: last orders | + BARKEEP'S NOTE | |
| UV: no witnesses | + CLEANING ROTA | |
| UV: dead mans hand | + GREEN ROOM | |
| UV: the vipers nest | + HIT LIST | |
| UV: blood money | + BLOOD MONEY | |
| Trade: Mobsters | + THE VIPER | − BARKEEP'S NOTE (proposed) |
| Trade: Thieves | + JADE DAGGER | − LIGHTER or BLOOD MONEY (proposed) |
| Trade: Occultists | + BLACK LEDGER | − TORN PAGE or MARKED CARDS (proposed) |
| Accusation prompt | | − THE VIPER, JADE DAGGER, GREEN ROOM, BLACK LEDGER, HIT LIST, BARKEEP'S NOTE, CLEANING ROTA |

**Problem with the above:** The detective trades away BARKEEP'S NOTE to the mobsters but also needs it for the accusation. This means either:
- (a) The trade happens *after* the accusation (mobsters get it as a reward for helping), or
- (b) The detective trades something else to the mobsters (BLOOD MONEY, MARKED CARDS), or
- (c) The prompt removes the clues only on success, and trading happens before — meaning the detective must get the traded clues, complete the accusation, THEN trade away the leftovers.

**Recommended approach:** Option (c) — the accusation comes first. The detective gathers everything, solves the case, and then trades away the surplus clues they no longer need. OR option (b) — the detective trades non-accusation clues (MARKED CARDS, BLOOD MONEY, TORN PAGE) and keeps all accusation clues. This is cleaner.

**Revised trade offers (option b):**

| Trade | Detective gives | Detective gets |
|---|---|---|
| Mobsters | BLOOD MONEY (financial evidence they want suppressed) | THE VIPER |
| Thieves | LIGHTER or NOTEBOOK | JADE DAGGER |
| Occultists | TORN PAGE + MARKED CARDS | BLACK LEDGER |

This way the detective never trades away a clue they need for the accusation.

## Open Questions

1. **Code phrase for accusation page** — Should `accusation` be typed directly, found via UV, or given by the ghost-host actor verbally as a reward for completing the investigation?
2. **Cross-faction trade timing** — Do all factions reach Part 3 at roughly the same time? Does the detective need to wait for others to progress?
3. **Moral tension** — The BLOOD MONEY trade to mobsters (suppressing financial evidence) is still morally loaded. Lean into it?
4. **What happens after `case closed`?** — Shared endgame across factions, or investigator-specific Part 4?
5. **Red herring count** — 3 herring pages + 3 atmospheric marks. Enough dead ends, or more?
6. **Marco Peretti** — The name on the `cold deck` red herring. Should this connect to another faction's storyline, or stay pure noise?
7. **JADE DAGGER physicality** — Should there be a physical prop dagger in the room that the thieves interact with?
