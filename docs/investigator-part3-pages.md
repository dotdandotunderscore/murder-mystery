## Page 42 — "The Hunt Begins"

**Code phrase:** `the hunt begins`
**Code phrase source:** Given verbally by ghost-host actor after live testimony scene

**Required flags:** `found the shell of mr. sage`, `the pale flame`
**Required flags hints:** `"You haven't found what you're looking for yet."`, `"You need a way to see what's hidden."`

**Grants flags:** `sage spoke`
**Grants clues:** TORN PAGE

**Content:**

> The dead have spoken. The pieces are scattered but the shape is forming — a private card game, a man they called the Viper, numbers that didn't add up, and a ledger that should have stayed closed.
>
> That's all the ghost could give you. Fragments. Echoes. The rest is on you.
>
> The Pale Flame hums in your hand. Madame Web said it reveals what the guilty try to hide — words written in ink only this light can show. Time to put that to the test.
>
> Search this place. Every surface, every shadow, every corner nobody looks at twice. The walls of the Broken Drum remember what the people inside it want you to forget.

**Prompt:** None
**Text links:** None (player must physically search with UV light)

## Page 43 — "The Viper's Table"

**Code phrase:** `snake eyes`
**Code phrase source:** UV ink on/under a playing card or card table

**Required flags:** `the pale flame`
**Required flags hints:** `"You see something strange, but you can't make it out."`

**Grants flags:** `found the viper`
**Grants clues:** MARKED CARDS

**Content:**

> The Pale Flame slides across the green felt and the table lights up like a confession.
>
> Tally marks. Dozens of them, scratched in clusters of five. Initials next to each cluster — winners who were never meant to win, losers who never had a chance. The system is elegant in its cruelty: marked cards, loaded dice, dealers who know exactly when to smile and when to sweep.
>
> The house wasn't just winning. The house was hunting.
>
> Sage wasn't a gambler. He was an accountant. He would have seen these numbers for what they were before his drink arrived. And the man who ran these tables — the one they called the Viper — would have seen Sage seeing them.
>
> You pocket a marked card as evidence. Wherever there are crooked cards, there are crooked drinks to go with them. Maybe the bar knows something.

**Prompt:** None
**Text links:** None

>
> You've got a witness. Or the next best thing.
>
> The barkeep saw him go in. Sage never came out. But where did they take what was left? There's always a door nobody uses — a way out that doesn't get talked about. Look for it.

**Required flags hints:** `"You see something strange, but you can't make it out."`

**Grants flags:** `saw the cleanup`

**Content:**

>
> Beneath them, a schedule. Dates. Rooms. Times. Not a cleaning rota for spilled drinks and cigarette burns — a cleaning rota for problems. The Green Room shows up three times in the last six months. Each entry followed by the same notation: *NW. Complete.*
>
> No witnesses. Complete.
>
> The night Sage died, the Green Room was scrubbed within the hour. Whatever weapon did the job was removed. The staff were gathered and informed, politely, that Mr. Sage had left the casino in good health and good spirits. Anyone who remembered differently would be helped to forget.
>
> Whoever cleaned that room missed the rug though. They always miss something. And somewhere in this place, a dead man left his own message. The dead remember things differently than the living — find a mirror.

## Page 46 — "The Last Hand"

**Code phrase:** `dead mans hand`
**Code phrase source:** UV ink on a mirror or reflective surface

**Required flags:** `the pale flame`
**Required flags hints:** `"You see something strange, but you can't make it out."`

**Grants flags:** `saw the last hand`
**Grants clues:** GREEN ROOM

**Content:**

> A mirror. Your own face stares back, tired and suspicious. But the Pale Flame shows something else — words on the glass, or behind it, or somewhere in between.
>
> Sage's last night, written in a dead man's hand.
>
> He came back to the Broken Drum with the torn page from the ledger tucked inside his jacket. Thought he was holding a winning hand — show the authorities what he'd found, or the casino pays for his silence. A simple transaction.
>
> He didn't understand what kind of establishment he was trying to rob. You don't blackmail people who solve problems by making people disappear. You especially don't do it in their own building, in their own room, on their own terms.
>
> He sat across from the Viper in the Green Room. Showed him the evidence. The Viper smiled.
>
> Sage didn't leave that room alive.
>
> You stare at the mirror. Your reflection stares back. For a moment — just a moment — you swear there's someone standing behind you. But when you turn around, there's nobody there.
>
> Something in this place has more of his secrets. Hidden where only a snake would go.

## Page 47 — "The Viper's Nest"

**Code phrase:** `the vipers nest`
**Code phrase source:** UV ink in a hard-to-find spot — ceiling, inside a drawer, under furniture

**Required flags:** `the pale flame`, `found the viper`
**Required flags hints:** `"You see something strange, but you can't make it out."`, `"You don't know what you're looking at yet."`

**Grants flags:** `knows the truth`
**Grants clues:** HIT LIST

**Content:**

> This one was hard to find. Whoever put it here didn't want it found. But the Pale Flame doesn't care about intentions.
>
> Initials: V.V. Vincent Vane. And beneath them, a list. Names, written in the same invisible ink, each one with a single line drawn through it. Eight names. Eight lines. Eight people who became problems and then became nothing.
>
> Mr. Sage is the seventh name on the list. The eighth is someone you don't recognise. The space below the eighth is empty and waiting.
>
> Vincent Vane doesn't just run rigged card games. He's the casino's fixer. When someone sees too much, knows too much, threatens the wrong people — the Viper handles it. The mob points, and the Viper bites. Quietly, efficiently, and in rooms that get cleaned before dawn.
>
> You have the full picture now. The motive: Sage found the ledger. The killer: Vincent Vane, the Viper. The method: a private meeting in the Green Room that was never meant to end with both men walking out.
>
> But knowing isn't proving. You need evidence. A name. A weapon. A book of sins. You don't have them — but someone in this casino does. Time to start trading favours.

**Prompt:** None
**Text links:** None

## Page 48 — "Follow the Money"

**Code phrase:** `blood money`
**Code phrase source:** UV ink on a wall, behind a picture frame

**Required flags:** `the pale flame`
**Required flags hints:** `"You see something strange, but you can't make it out."`

**Grants flags:** `followed the money`
**Grants clues:** BLOOD MONEY

**Content:**

> Behind the frame, the wall is alive with numbers. The Pale Flame reveals columns of figures — dates, amounts, names. A shadow ledger, written where no auditor would ever think to look. Not even a particularly paranoid one.
>
> The Broken Drum was washing dirty money through the private games. Phantom winners collecting real payouts. Real losses routed to accounts that don't exist in any bank you've heard of. A laundromat in a tuxedo.
>
> Sage saw it. Accountant's eyes. He couldn't help himself — it's like asking a musician not to hear a wrong note. The numbers were screaming and he was the only one in the room who spoke the language.
>
> He took a page. One page from the real ledger. That was all it took. One page, and a man who thought proof was the same thing as power.
>
> The money flows through this place like blood through a body. Dirty in, clean out. And anyone who notices the stain ends up on the Viper's list.

## Page 52 — "The Case of Mr. Sage"

**Code phrase:** `accusation`
**Code phrase source:** Given verbally by ghost-host actor when detective has `knows the truth` flag and is ready

**Required flags:** `sage spoke`, `knows the truth`
**Required flags hints:** `"You're not ready to make an accusation yet."`, `"You haven't found the full truth yet."`

**Grants flags:** `case closed`
**Removes clues:** THE VIPER, JADE DAGGER, GREEN ROOM, BLACK LEDGER, HIT LIST, BARKEEP'S NOTE, CLEANING ROTA

**Content:**

> Three days ago a woman sat across your desk and asked you to find her husband. She was beautiful and she was scared and she was paying in cash. You said yes because that's what you do. You find people.
>
> You found him.
>
> You found him face-down on a rug in a private room in a casino that runs on crooked cards and dirty money. You found him because a woman who reads tarot in a basement gave you a torch that shows what the guilty try to hide. You found him because his ghost — his actual ghost, and you're going to need a lot of whiskey to process that part — spoke to you through a stranger's mouth.
>
> And now you know.
>
> You know who. You know how. You know where. You know why. You know he wasn't the first and you know he won't be the last unless somebody does something about it.
>
> Somebody. Meaning you. Because nobody else in this building is going to lift a finger.
>
> Time to lay your cards on the table, detective. Everything you've found, everything you've traded for, everything the Pale Flame showed you in the dark. One accusation. Make it count.

**Prompt:** `_____ killed Mr. Sage with the _____ in the _____ because he found the _____. The _____ proves this wasn't his first victim. The _____ places him at the scene. And the _____ shows how they made it all disappear.`

**Answer:** THE VIPER, JADE DAGGER, GREEN ROOM, BLACK LEDGER, HIT LIST, BARKEEP'S NOTE, CLEANING ROTA

**Wrong answer hints:**

| Wrong answer | Hint text |
|---|---|
| TORN PAGE (in "found" slot) | `Close — but a single page isn't what got him killed. It's what it was torn from.` |
| VINCENT VANE (in killer slot) | `That's his real name. But that's not what they call him down here. What do they call a man who strikes without warning?` |
| MARKED CARDS (in any slot) | `The cards are how it started. But they're not what Sage was killed for.` |
| BLOOD MONEY (in motive slot) | `The money is part of it. But Sage found something more specific — something with names and numbers and proof.` |
| NOTEBOOK (in any slot) | `Your notebook is full of notes. But this prompt needs evidence, not observations.` |
| PENCIL (in any slot) | `A pencil isn't going to solve a murder, detective.` |
| LIGHTER (in any slot) | `You use that to light cigarettes, not build cases.` |
| Any red herring name | `Dead end. You already know that one leads nowhere.` |

**Prompt success text:**

> The pieces fall into place like a hand you were always meant to be dealt.
>
> The Viper killed Mr. Sage. Jade dagger, Green Room, one clean thrust. Sage had found the Black Ledger — proof the Broken Drum was washing money through rigged games — and made the mistake of thinking proof was leverage. The hit list shows Sage wasn't the first problem Vane solved for the house. The barkeep saw him go in and never come out. And the cleaning rota proves the casino scrubbed the scene before the blood was dry.
>
> Case closed. You know the truth.
>
> Whether anyone else will believe you — a private detective with a magic torch and a ghost's testimony — that's another problem for another night.
