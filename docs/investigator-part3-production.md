# Investigator Part 3 — Production & Cross-Faction Reference

This document covers everything needed to set up and run Part 3 that isn't page content: physical room setup, actor briefing, cross-faction dependencies, and hooks for other storylines.

---

## Physical Room: UV Ink Setup

Write these in UV-reactive ink around the venue. Players find them using the Pale Flame (UV torch given in Part 2).

### Code Phrases (Real Investigation — 6 marks)

| Code Phrase | Where to Write It | Placement Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `snake eyes` | On or under a playing card, or on the card table surface | Easiest to find — this is the entry point. Place where players will naturally shine the light first. |
| `last orders` | On or near the bar, on a drinks menu, or on the bar surface | Should be discoverable by anyone examining the bar area. |
| `no witnesses` | Near a doorway or exit sign | On the frame, on the wall beside it, or on the door itself. |
| `dead mans hand` | On a mirror or reflective surface | On the glass itself — the Pale Flame will reveal it when shone at the mirror. |
| `the vipers nest` | Hard-to-find spot: ceiling, inside a drawer, under furniture, behind something | This should be the hardest UV clue to find. Reward thorough searchers. |
| `blood money` | On a wall, behind a picture frame, or on a poster | Visible when the player shines the light on the wall / lifts the frame. |

### Code Phrases (Red Herrings — 5 marks)

| Code Phrase | Where to Write It |
|---|---|
| `lucky streak` | Near a slot machine, roulette wheel, or gaming area |
| `house rules` | On a notice board or near a staff/service door |
| `cold deck` | On the underside of a chair or table |
| `high roller` | Near a velvet rope or VIP area |
| `after hours` | Near a clock or staff area |

### Atmospheric Marks (Not Code Phrases — 3 marks)

| Mark | Where to Write It | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| `V.V.` | Scratched near a doorframe | Vincent Vane's initials. Sharp players will connect this to the Viper later. |
| A column of numbers (e.g. `12,400 / 8,750 / 31,200 / 5,600`) | On a wall behind a picture | Looks like financial records. Reinforces the money laundering angle. |
| A name crossed out (e.g. ~~`HARGREAVES`~~) | Under a table or on a chair | Implies Sage wasn't the first victim. Ominous flavour. |

### Total UV Marks: 14

- 6 real code phrases (unlock investigation pages with clues)
- 5 red herring code phrases (unlock dead-end pages)
- 3 atmospheric (flavour only, no pages)

## Ghost-Host Actor Briefing

### Who They Are

A staff member or actor who plays the host of Mr. Sage's ghost. The detective identified them via the Pale Flame at the end of Part 2. They are the primary live interaction point for investigators in Part 3.

### Initial Scene (Triggered by Player)

When an investigator approaches and says "Sage" (or similar — "Mr. Sage", "I'm looking for Sage", etc.):

1. **Perform the ghost scene.** Go glassy-eyed, change your voice — deeper, older, afraid. Speak in fragments:
   - *"The cards... the cards were wrong..."*
   - *"A man with cold eyes... they called him... the Viper..."*
   - *"Numbers. I found the numbers. In the book. I shouldn't have taken it..."*
   - *"Green walls. Green room. He smiled at me. Then..."*
   - Trail off. Blink. Come back to yourself. Look confused.

2. **Give them the code phrase.** Tell the player (out of character or in character): *"Type 'the hunt begins' into the app."*

### Ongoing Hints (After Initial Scene)

Players can return at any time for guidance. Check their progress (ask a game runner, or ask the player what they've found so far) and nudge accordingly:

| Player seems... | Say something like... |
|---|---|
| Lost, hasn't found any UV clues | *"The cards... start at the tables... the light will show you..."* |
| Has found the Viper but not the barkeep | *"Someone saw... at the bar... they watched him leave and me stay..."* |
| Has found a few clues, missing the mirror | *"The dead remember in reflections... find a mirror..."* |
| Has found most clues, missing the nest | *"His den... he keeps trophies of what he's done... look where you wouldn't think to..."* |
| Has all the evidence, needs to trade | *"You know what happened. But knowing isn't enough. You need proof. The weapon. The ledger. His name. Others here have what you need..."* |
| Is stuck on a red herring | *"That's not the way... you're chasing shadows... go back to what you know..."* |
| Has everything, ready for accusation | Give them the code phrase: *"accusation"* |

### General Demeanour

Fragmented, pained, fading in and out. Speak in short bursts. Not fully aware of being dead — slip between memories and the present. When not actively performing, just be a normal staff member. The ghost only surfaces when investigators approach.

## Cross-Faction Dependencies

### Clues the Investigator Needs from Other Factions

These three clues are required for the final accusation prompt. Each faction must grant this clue somewhere in their Part 3 progression so that it becomes available for trade.

| Clue | From Faction | Why That Faction Has It | Suggested Encounter |
|---|---|---|---|
| **THE VIPER** | Mobsters | Vincent Vane is one of theirs — his codename or identity surfaces in their storyline | Perhaps Vane is their handler, or they're ordered to work with/for him. The clue THE VIPER is granted as part of learning who runs the casino's enforcement. |
| **JADE DAGGER** | Thieves | Found during their heist — a distinctive jade-handled dagger stashed somewhere in the casino | An item they steal or find. It's just loot to them — they don't know it's a murder weapon. No physical prop needed. |
| **BLACK LEDGER** | Occultists | The ledger has significance beyond finances — it records occult debts, soul-bindings, or spiritual transactions | Discovered through supernatural investigation. Possibly connected to why Sage's ghost is tethered to the host. |

### Clues the Investigator Can Trade Away

These clues are earned by the investigator and are NOT needed for the accusation prompt. They are available as trade currency.

| Clue | Earned From | Suggested Trade Partner | Why They'd Want It |
|---|---|---|---|
| **TORN PAGE** | Ghost scene (page 42) | Occultists | A fragment of the ledger — they want it to complete their understanding of the occult debt system |
| **MARKED CARDS** | UV: `snake eyes` (page 43) | Any faction | Evidence of rigged games — could advance any faction's understanding of the casino |
| **BLOOD MONEY** | UV: `blood money` (page 48) | Mobsters | Financial evidence the mob might want suppressed — or leverage for another faction |
| **LIGHTER** | Part 1 (shelter) | Thieves | A practical tool — the thief might need fire for something in their storyline |
| **NOTEBOOK** | Start of game | Any faction | Information is currency |
| **PENCIL** | Start of game | Any faction | Bundle with notebook or standalone trade |

### Suggested Trade Pairings

| Investigator gives | Investigator gets | Trade partner |
|---|---|---|
| BLOOD MONEY | THE VIPER | Mobsters |
| LIGHTER or NOTEBOOK | JADE DAGGER | Thieves |
| TORN PAGE | BLACK LEDGER | Occultists |

These are suggestions — actual trades happen organically between players. The key requirement is that each faction's Part 3 grants the clue the investigator needs, and the investigator has enough surplus clues to offer something in return.

## Hooks for Other Factions

### Names & Entities Other Factions Can Reference

| Name | What It Is | Available For |
|---|---|---|
| **Vincent Vane / The Viper** | Casino fixer who killed Sage. Runs rigged private games. Has a hit list. | Mobster storyline (he's one of theirs), Thief storyline (his office or belongings could be a heist target) |
| **The Green Room** | Private room where Sage was killed | Any faction — it's a location in the casino |
| **The Black Ledger** | Shadow financial records / occult debt book | Occultist storyline (primary), Mobster storyline (they'd want it protected) |
| **Marco Peretti** | A name found on a UV red herring (`cold deck` page). No connection to the murder — but the name exists and could be used by another faction's storyline if desired. Currently a dead end for investigators. | Any faction — free to repurpose |
| **Delacroix** | High-roller on a winning streak. Red herring for investigators. | Any faction — could be a contact, a mark, a patron |
| **The Barkeep** | Unnamed witness who saw the Viper enter the Green Room with Sage. Left a message in UV ink. | Mobster storyline (they'd want to find/silence this person) |
| **Madame Web** | Tarot reader with genuine eldritch powers. Gave the investigator the Pale Flame. Knows more than she says. | Occultist storyline (she's connected to the supernatural layer), any faction |

### The Investigator's Endgame Flag

When the investigator completes the accusation, they receive the flag `case closed`. This flag can be used as a prerequisite for shared endgame content.

### Timeline

All factions are expected to reach Part 3 at approximately the same time. The investigator's trading phase depends on other factions having progressed far enough to hold THE VIPER, JADE DAGGER, and BLACK LEDGER. If one faction is behind, the investigator may need to wait — the ghost-host actor can stall by continuing to give UV search hints.
